# Customer Onboarding Guide

## Step 1: Kickoff call
Schedule a kickoff with the customer success manager, technical lead, and support manager. Confirm business goals, expected volumes, and launch timeline.

## Step 2: Configure workspace
Set up the workspace, knowledge base, and ticket routing rules. Add the customer-specific macros, escalation flows, and product permissions.

## Step 3: Train support agents
Provide a guided walkthrough on using the AI drafting assistant, escalation rules, and case triage workflow. Focus on policy clarity and review checkpoints.

## Step 4: Launch with a pilot
Run a limited pilot on a subset of ticket types. Review response quality, SLA adherence, and customer feedback before scaling.
