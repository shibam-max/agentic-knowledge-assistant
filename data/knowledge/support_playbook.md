# Support Playbook

## Escalation process
When a customer issue impacts SLA, security, or compliance, escalate to the engineering lead and customer success lead immediately.

## Common support issues
- Incomplete workflow automation
- Incorrect routing of customer tickets
- Slow updates to the knowledge base
- Poor onboarding documentation

## Recommended actions
Use the product overview, training guide, and escalation policy together to decide the best response path. Summarize findings, confirm business impact, and define the next operational step.
