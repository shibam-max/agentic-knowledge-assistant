# Product Overview

## Mission
Our product helps teams automate recurring customer support tasks, reduce manual work, and improve response consistency.

## Core features
- AI-assisted drafts for support tickets
- Workflows to assign issues to the right team
- Knowledge-base retrieval for customer support agents
- Analytics for SLA tracking and trend monitoring

## Quarterly priorities
- Improve agent onboarding speed
- Increase resolution quality for complex support cases
- Expand integration support with ticketing systems
